Top 5 Legit Apps to Earn Money in Bangladesh (2026)

By MoneyCore Team • November 19, 2026 • 5 min read
[ Ad Space - Responsive Banner ]

Many people in Bangladesh search for online earning apps, but most of them end up wasting their time on fake or scam apps. In this article, we will show you the top 5 legit apps that actually pay you via bKash or Nagad.

1. Fiverr (Freelancing App)

Fiverr is not a "click and earn" app, but it is the most reliable way to earn real money. You can sell your skills like graphic design, translation, or data entry. Once you set up your profile, you can earn hundreds of dollars per month.

2. Upwork

Similar to Fiverr, Upwork allows you to create a profile and bid on jobs. It's perfect for those who have skills but want to work on an hourly basis.

3. Toloka (Yandex Toloka)

Toloka is a micro-task app where you can earn money by completing small tasks like image annotation or text evaluation. It pays in USD, and you can withdraw directly to your bank or use a virtual card.

4. Remotasks

If you are interested in AI and tech, Remotasks is a great platform. You can label images, text, and audio data for AI training. Payment is usually sent via PayPal or AirTM.

5. Cash App / Bitcoin Faucets (Caution Advised)

While there are many apps promising to pay you for watching videos or clicking ads (like TimeBucks), the earnings are usually very low. If you want to use them, treat them as a side hustle, not a main income source.

Conclusion

The truth is, there is no easy way to earn money online without skills. The best investment is to learn a skill (like web development, graphic design, or digital marketing) and sell it on platforms like Fiverr or Upwork.
